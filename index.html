at Oakhaven Light

We believe in a faith that’s active, a love that’s inclusive, and a
community that supports you. Wherever you are on your
journey - whether you’re just exploring or have walked this path
for years - you’re welcome here.
​
At Oakhaven Light, community is about real connection.
Through small groups, service, and worship, we create space to
grow, belong, and find purpose. Every story matters.
​
You don’t have to do life alone. Here, you’ll find people who
stand with you, support you, and grow alongside you in faith.

Grounded in Grace

Our core beliefs center on love, community, and hope. We walk
alongside you in faith, offering encouragement and compassion in
every season, grounded in a grace that lifts and unites us. ​

We believe grace is a gift - freely given, renewing hearts and bringing
strength in both joy and struggle. Through worship, prayer, and shared
experiences, we grow closer to God and to one another. ​

Together, we create a space where everyone feels seen, valued, and
loved. Here, you’re not alone - you’re part of a community grounded in
grace, moving forward with hope and purpose.
